Pumperly is an open-source fuel and EV route planner with live prices from 22 countries. Pick a destination and it draws the route, lists every station along it with its price and the minutes a stop would add, and plans the cheapest places to fill up.

The app shows pumperly.com and adds parts that work without the site:
- A home-screen widget with the cheapest stations near you for your fuel, in 2x2 or 4x2. It never asks for background location.
- A "Cheapest nearby" screen: touch and hold the app icon.
- When you are offline, the last known stations, each with a button to open it in your map app.
- A fuel setting, chosen on first launch and changed from the widget or the app icon.

Also: Spanish and English, dark mode, pumperly.com links open in the app, and a release APK of about 2 MB with no Google Play services, no ads and no tracking.

Prices come from official government feeds and community sources. Pumperly is free software under the GPL.
